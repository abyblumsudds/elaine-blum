Hi, I'm Elaine!

I am a neurodiverse- and queer-affirming therapist that specializes in engaging where these things intersect (and where they don't). In a previous life, I earned a PhD in philosophy, specializing in queer and trans theory. I pivoted to focus this knowledge towards more directly supporting LGBTQIA2S+ communities through mental health support.

Learn more about me here!

How can I help you?

  • Assessing for and supporting ADHD and/or Autism
  • Gender-affirming care (including letter-writing)
  • EMDR re-processing for trauma
  • Unlearning ableist, neuro-normative, heteronormative, transphobic, and other oppressive narratives
  • Balancing academic demands (perfectionism, job market stress, work/life balance)

These are just a few of my specialties. Learn more about my services here!
